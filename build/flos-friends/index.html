Vets, physiotherapists and groomers I work alongside

Behaviour rarely sits in one place, so I don’t work in isolation.

  • Lily Moffatt Veterinary Physiotherapy

    Veterinary physiotherapy is science-based and takes a holistic approach to each animal, with clinical reasoning behind treatment on a case-by-case basis following veterinary referral. It works alongside veterinary care in the treatment and long-term management of musculoskeletal and neurological conditions, with the aim of reducing pain, improving movement and restoring normal muscle function.

  • Nicholson Vets

    Independent, founder-led practice in Newark, opened in 2026 by Conor Nicholson. A named vet each visit, unhurried consultations, on-site CT and their own out-of-hours service.

  • K9 Corner Dog Grooming

    Grooming doesn’t come without stress for some dogs, but it is necessary for their welfare. Their ethos is caring and compassionate, every dog is treated with respect, minimal restraint is used in a calming environment, and only natural and sustainable products are used.

  • Maddy’s Mutts

    Secure dog field, for dogs who need space away from other dogs.
