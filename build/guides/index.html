GuidesPractical guides for owners of nervous, anxious and reactive dogs.

Written once and kept up to date, rather than a blog. Each one answers a question I get asked in consultations.

  • The Tellington TTouch harness for nervous dogs

    How the Tellington TTouch harness changed my nervous dog's walks — what it is, why the two connection points matter, and how to fit one.

  • Is your dog's behaviour caused by pain?

    Bunny hopping, a shortened stride, refusing food near a trigger, broken sleep. The physical signs behind behaviour that looks like fear or reactivity.

  • Enrichment and freework for anxious dogs

    Scatter feeding, foraging mats and ACE freework — how sniffing lowers arousal, and what to try first with a nervous dog.

  • Muzzle training without the stigma

    Why a muzzle is a kindness for some dogs, how to introduce one so your dog likes it, and choosing a shape that lets them pant and drink.

  • Long lines and decompression walks

    Why a five-metre line beats a retractable lead, where to walk a dog who cannot cope with other dogs, and how sniffing brings arousal down.

  • Tug toys and building confidence

    Letting your dog win, why tug is not about dominance, and how play builds the engagement that nervous dogs need on a walk.

  • Calming products for anxious dogs: what actually helps

    Pet Remedy, plug-ins, sprays and bandanas — what the evidence supports, what to expect, and what no product can do on its own.

  • Natural chews and why chewing calms dogs

    Chewing releases stress, but it can also signal pain. What to offer, what to avoid, and what a sudden increase in chewing may mean.

  • Dogs from overseas: Romanian and imported rescue dogs

    What is different about a dog who arrived from abroad, why the first months matter, and how to build safety before asking for anything.
