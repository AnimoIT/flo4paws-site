1. Getting started

Setting up takes three short steps: fill in Flo's form, set a password from the email that follows, then sign in.

Fill in the form

The form has five steps: About you, Your animals, About each dog, Consent and Review. Select Continue to move on, or Back to change an earlier step. The panel underneath (at the side on a computer) shows who and what the form covers so far. Your answers are kept in your browser as you go, so you can come back to the form; consents are not kept.

  • About you. Your name and email address are needed; Flo uses your email to set up your account. You can also say when you are usually free.
  • Your animals. Each dog's name and what kind of animal they are, and their breed if you know it. Select Add another dog for more than one.
  • About each dog. Their background, the behaviour you'd like help with, their health and vet, and their daily life. Answer what you can.
  • Consent. Read each one and answer Yes or No. A few are needed before the form can be sent.
  • Review. Check your answers, then select Send my details.

Once it is sent you'll see a thank-you page, and Flo will be in touch.

Already a client of Flo4Paws? Flo may send you a link to a shorter form, just for joining the app: it asks for your contact details, the basics about your dogs, your vet and the consents. The steps after it are the same.

Set your password

Straight after you send the form you get an email, Complete your Flo4Paws registration. Open the link in it; it works for 7 days (check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived). Choose a password of 8 to 128 characters with at least one letter and one number, type it twice and select Create my account. Then select Sign in to use the app in your browser, or Get the iPhone app or Get the Android app. See how it works in 30 seconds opens a short getting-started page. If the link has run out, ask Flo for a new one; it comes as Your new Flo4Paws registration link, and any earlier link stops working.

Signing in

Open the app, or go to app.flo4paws.co.uk, and sign in with your email address and password. In the iPhone or Android app you're offered fingerprint or face unlock after signing in; signing out turns it off.

Forgotten your password? Select Forgot your password?, enter your email and select Send Reset Link. The email, Reset your Flo4Paws password, has a link that works for 7 days. This is also how to change your password. (To stop misuse, reset emails are limited to a few a day.)

2. Home

Home is the first screen after you sign in. It shows what matters now, in this order; parts with nothing to show are left out.

  • Hello, and how many of your diary entries and sessions are waiting for Flo4Paws to review (or "You're all up to date").
  • Your animals: if you have more than one, a button for each. Home opens on the first with a plan; tap another to see theirs.
  • Next up: your next appointment, with when, what, who with and which dog, and Booked. An appointment that is part of your package says Part of your package, with no price. If there is a price you'll see it, with To pay or ✓ Paid. Use Add to calendar, or Change or cancel to open it (an appointment that can no longer be changed in the app has Details instead).
  • Your plan: the homework and exercises Flo has set for the dog you've chosen (see Your plan).
  • Quick actions: start a Diary entry or Send a message, and Gait assessment.
  • Recent diary: the last three entries and sessions (yours and any co-owner's), each with its number of replies, or Waiting, Reviewed or Flagged. See all opens the Diary.
  • Later: up to three more appointments, and All appointments: past, cancelled and requests.

The bar at the bottom takes you to Home, Diary, Media, Messages and Profile. The 🔔 bell at the top shows your notifications; a red number counts the new ones.

3. Your plan

Your plan on Home holds what Flo has set for your dog between sessions.

  • Homework: a list of things to work on. If Flo has attached a handout, it shows under the item as a 📄 chip: tap it to open it. More than three items fold under Read more. When Flo marks an item done it moves under Done (N); tap that to see them, with the day each was done. With nothing left open it says "All your homework is done. Nice work."
  • Exercises: each shows how many and how often, and when you last logged it (not logged yet in orange). Tap an exercise to see its description, Flo's notes, Watch the video, and your last three sessions with their status and replies (tap one to open it).

When Flo adds homework, rewords an item, attaches a handout, or adds an exercise, or a physio adds one, you (and any co-owners) get an email and a notification, "<name> at Flo4Paws has updated <dog>'s plan", about 10 minutes after the last change.

Log a session

Select Log beside an exercise after you've done it. Choose the time of day, add a note on how it went if you like, and up to 10 photos or videos (up to 100 MB each, about 105 MB in all), then Save session. If the photos fail to upload the session is still saved; photos can't be added to it afterwards.

Flo is told. When Flo replies you get "New exercise session reply", and the session is marked reviewed; if Flo reviews it you get "Your exercise session has been reviewed" (or flagged). A physio can reply too; a physio's reply doesn't mark it reviewed.

If there are no exercises but there is homework, the homework has a Tell us how it went button that opens a diary entry instead.

4. Care from LM Vet Physio

If Flo refers your dog to LM Vet Physio and you agree to share care, you keep using this app. Lily at LM Vet Physio sets exercises and homework in her own practice's app, and they appear in your plan here marked From LM Vet Physio, with their video links and handouts (a very large handout, over 10 MB, can't be sent: ask Lily for it).

  • Log sessions of those exercises with Log as usual. Your session (when, how it went and your ratings), and the messages about it, go to LM Vet Physio, and Lily's replies appear in the session's Discussion as LM Vet Physio. Photos or videos you add to a session stay with Flo4Paws and don't reach LM Vet Physio: send those to Flo, or ask Lily.
  • When LM Vet Physio adds to or rewords the plan you get a notification, "LM Vet Physio has updated <dog>'s plan", and an email, "<dog>'s plan has been updated". Their replies and reviews reach you by email and under the bell, from Flo4Paws.
  • Flo4Paws can see everything too, and can tick LM Vet Physio's homework done. Your messages to Flo, and the shared thread, stay with Flo4Paws.
  • Either practice can stop sharing at any time. LM Vet Physio's exercises and open homework then leave your plan; sessions you logged stay in your diary.

5. Appointments

Your next appointment is on Home. All appointments: past, cancelled and requests opens the full list: Upcoming, your five most recent Previous ones, and a folded Cancelled and not accepted list. A past appointment with Notes available has Flo's notes from the session; open it to read them.

Open an appointment to see the date and time, who with, where, which dog, the price and its notes. Appointments from the calendar have three buttons until they start (for anything else, contact Flo):

  • Add to calendar saves it to your phone or computer calendar.
  • Ask to change opens Messages with a note about the appointment, ready for you to say what would suit.
  • Cancel asks you to confirm. Less than 48 hours before, it warns you that a late-cancellation charge may apply. You can give a reason and a note (up to 500 characters). Flo is told straight away, and you get an email confirming it.

To book or move an appointment, send Flo a message. If you have a package, appointments under it say Part of your package and have no price of their own; Flo will tell you how to pay for the package.

6. Your diary

Your diary is where you tell Flo how things have gone between sessions. Select Diary (or Diary entry on Home).

  • Choose the dog, the date and the time of day.
  • Under Event, say what happened. Add the Trigger (what set it off) and the Reaction (what your dog did).
  • If Flo has set rating scales for your dog, rate each one from Very Low to Very High; Flo may have described each level. Every scale shown needs a rating.
  • Add any Details, and photos or videos with Attach Photos / Videos (up to 10 files; videos and large photos up to 1 GB each, smaller photos up to about 105 MB in all).
  • Select Submit Entry. If you have already logged an entry for that dog at that time of day today, the app asks before adding another.

Your entries are listed under it, with any co-owner's marked by <name>. Each shows Pending until Flo has seen it, then Reviewed, or Flagged if Flo wants to follow something up. Open an entry to see its photos and videos, Flo's note, and the Discussion: type in Write a reply… and select Send. You can edit or delete your own replies.

You can ✎ Edit the event, trigger, reaction and details of your own entry until Flo has marked it reviewed (a flagged entry can still be edited); it goes back to pending. Entries can't be deleted in the app; ask Flo if one needs removing.

If a big video or photo stops uploading, the entry says so: open it and select Resume upload to carry on where it stopped. If you have closed the app since, select + Add photos or videos on the entry and choose the same file. + Add photos or videos also adds more to an entry later.

7. Photos, videos and notes

Select Media to see the photos and videos you and Flo have shared (those attached to diary entries stay with the entry). Filter by All, Photos, Videos, Pending or Reviewed. To add some, select + Add Media, choose the files and the dog, and select Upload (up to 10 files; videos up to 1 GB, and a stopped upload carries on if you pick the same file again). 🗑 on something you uploaded deletes it, after asking; you can't delete it once Flo has shared it (with your vet, say) or it is part of a gait assessment you have sent, or delete what Flo added.

Vet & physio notes keeps letters and reports about your dog, on two tabs: Vet notes and Physio notes, each with a count. Choose the dog and the tab, then + Add vet note or + Add physio note to upload a PDF or photo (up to 20 MB). Flo sees it straight away, and notes Flo or a physio adds appear here too. Tap a name to open it; 🗑 deletes one you added.

8. Gait assessment

Flo may ask you to record a gait assessment before you meet: 8 short videos and 4 photos of your dog that show how they move. Open it from Gait assessment in Quick actions on Home, or in Profile, or from the link in Flo's email, "Flo4Paws: please record <dog>'s gait videos and photos" (the link works without signing in, for 14 days).

  • Choose the dog (if you have more than one) and select Start.
  • For each item, select Record or choose a video or Take or choose a photo: walking away from the camera, towards it, left side and right side to it; sit to stand and lie to stand (at least 6 times each); give paw (at least 4 times); running free off lead or on a long line; and photos from the front, both sides and behind. A tick shows each one is saved; Replace or Remove changes it.
  • Film in good light on a flat, non-slip surface, holding the camera steady at your dog's height. Videos can be up to 1 GB.
  • You don't have to do it all at once: what you upload is kept. When all 12 are in, select Send to my practitioner. Flo is told, and the screen says when you sent it.

The videos and photos also appear in your Media. Once sent, a set can't be changed; you can start a new one later if Flo asks.

9. Messages

Select Messages to message Flo. Type and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line), or attach a file with 📎 (up to 25 MB). ✓✓ under your message means Flo has read it. A red number on Messages counts new replies.

A dog with a co-owner (or a physio Flo has added to the conversation) has a shared thread. Choose Practice for your own conversation with Flo, or 🐾 <dog> for the shared one. Everyone caring for the dog, and Flo4Paws, sees the shared thread; it says who at the top. (Read ticks show only in your own conversation with Flo.)

10. Your profile

Select Profile (or your initials at the top) to see your counts and:

  • My Diary and My Media.
  • My Health Forms: the forms you've sent, with your answers and the consents you agreed to.
  • Submit New Health Form: Flo's health and behaviour form for a dog (three steps: About you, Your dog, Behaviour).
  • Comfort & Movement Assessment: a check of your dog's comfort and posture. You add 11 sets of photos and short videos (from behind, the front, above, the side, the feet, toileting, eating and the nose, and walking and shaking; up to 100 MB each) and answer 20 questions. If you stop part-way, your answers are kept on that device for 7 days; photos and videos need adding again.
  • Gait assessment: record the videos and photos Flo asks for (see Gait assessment).
  • Notifications, and Sign Out.

You can't change your name, email or address in the app: ask Flo and they will update it. To change your password, use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen.
