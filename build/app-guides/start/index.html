Getting started

How to get the app, sign in, and use it between sessions. The short video shows it all in about 30 seconds.

Step by step

  1. Get the app. On an iPhone, open the App Store; on Android, open Google Play. Search for Flo4Paws and install it. App Store · Google Play No phone handy? Everything also works in a web browser at app.flo4paws.co.uk.
  2. Set your password. After you send the health and history form, you get an email with a link to set your password. Check your spam folder if it has not arrived.
  3. Sign in. Open the app and sign in with your email address and that password. Forgotten it? Tap Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen.
  4. Home. Your next visit, and your dog’s plan: the homework Flo sets. Tap Tell us how it went when you have tried it.
  5. Keep the diary. A few lines about what happened, what set it off and how your dog reacted. Flo reads your entries and replies in the same place.
  6. Photos and videos. Use Media for a walk, a moment at the window, anything worth showing Flo.
  7. Message Flo. Questions between visits? Use Messages.

Want more detail? The client guide walks through every screen. Sam, Bramble and the messages in the video are examples.
