Physio guideWorking a Flo4Paws caseload in the app

For physiotherapists working with Flo4Paws clients: your caseload, prescribing exercises from your library, following the owner’s sessions, and what you can see.

1. Being added to a case

Flo adds you to the app as a physio and links you to each dog you work with. Flo sends you a welcome email with a link to set your password (it works for 7 days); then sign in at app.flo4paws.co.uk or in the Flo4Paws app. Flo can also let you into a dog's shared thread (see Messages).

Back to top

2. Your caseload

Home lists My Caseload: each dog you are linked to, with its owner. Tap one to open it. The bar at the bottom has Home, Diary, Media and Profile.

Back to top

3. A dog's page

Tap a dog to open its page. It lists every exercise prescribed for it (by you or by Flo), with reps or time, frequency, notes and the video. Below are the dog's Shared thread, if Flo has let you in, and Client activity: the exercise sessions the owner has logged.

Back to top

4. Prescribing

Select + Prescribe a resource. Choose a resource from your library, add reps or time, frequency and notes for the owner (all optional), and select Prescribe. The owner sees it in their plan; they and any co-owners get "<dog>'s plan has been updated" from "<your first name> at Flo4Paws" about 10 minutes after your last change.

Your library is your own: the picker lists only resources you have added. To add one here, select + Add a new resource to my library and give it a name (each name once), and if you like a description and a video link starting http:// or https://. If your library is empty, the sheet opens on that form.

You can't change or remove a prescription in the app yet; ask Flo.

Back to top

5. Your library

Profile › Manage my library lists your resources. + Add a resource adds one, Edit changes it and Remove takes it out of the library; anything you have already prescribed keeps working. Tick Show removed resources to bring one back with ↩ Restore.

Back to top

6. Client activity and replies

When the owner logs a session of any of the dog's exercises, it appears under Client activity on the dog's page, and you get an email, "New exercise session from <whoever logged it>". Select Open discussion to read it, type in Reply to the client… and select Send reply. The person who logged it gets "New exercise session reply from <your name>". Your reply doesn't mark the session reviewed; Flo does that.

The Diary tab shows the latest 20 diary entries and sessions for your dogs; choose a dog under Viewing dog. You can open one and read its discussion, but you reply to sessions from the dog's page.

Back to top

7. Media and notes

The Media tab shows your dogs' latest photos and videos (those attached to diary entries are inside the entries), and their Vet & physio notes on two tabs. You can open and read them; Flo or the owner adds them. If you have a report for a dog, send it to Flo.

Back to top

8. Messages

Physios don't have a private Messages tab. If Flo has let you into a dog's shared thread, it appears on the dog's page: the owner, any co-owners, you and Flo all see it. Use it for things everyone caring for the dog should know.

The shared thread sits above Client activity on the dog's page (top of the Client activity picture above).

Back to top

9. What the owner sees

Your exercises appear in Your plan on the owner's Home, next to Flo's homework. They tap an exercise for your description, notes and video, and Log to record a session.

Back to top

10. Emails you'll receive

EmailWhen
New exercise session from <name>The owner or a co-owner logs a session of an exercise
New diary reply / New exercise session replyThe owner or a co-owner replies on an entry or session for one of your dogs
Welcome to Flo4Paws — set your password / Reset your Flo4Paws passwordWhen Flo sends your welcome, or you ask to reset it

These also appear under the 🔔 bell, with new diary entries and shared-thread posts (Flo has the emails for those switched off for physios). Opening the bell marks them read.

What physios can't do

Physios can't edit or delete their replies, write diary entries, log sessions, upload photos or notes, see appointments or homework, send private messages, or reply on behaviour diary entries. Ask Flo for anything you need there.
