Rufford Park and Sherwood ForestWide woodland trails at Rufford Park and Sherwood Forest, with space to step off the path. Busier at weekends, so go early. Parking and terrain notes.

Distance10 miles
Route typeLoop
TerrainForest trails, countryside paths, quiet lanes
StartNear Ollerton or Wellow
NoteLeads required in places

This route covers Sherwood Forest National Nature Reserve and extends out towards Ollerton and Wellow. It sits near Rufford Country Park, but the walk itself goes further into Sherwood Forest and loops through the nearby villages.

Route overview

  • Start near Ollerton or Wellow
  • Heading north into Sherwood Forest NNR, famous for its ancient oaks including the Major Oak
  • Forest trails following well-trodden footpaths and bridleways with good woodland views
  • Returning south towards Wellow and the surrounding fields, back to the start

Terrain and what it is like underfoot

  • A mixture of forest trails, countryside paths and quiet lanes
  • Generally flat, with some uneven ground through the woodland sections
  • Good for walking, running and cycling — so expect to meet bikes
  • Dog-friendly, but leads are needed in places because of wildlife and livestock

Honest notes for a nervous dog

Woodland is a mixed blessing. It is quieter and there is more to sniff, but the sight lines are short and things appear around corners without warning — including bikes, which move fast and quietly.

Go early. It is considerably busier at weekends and in the afternoon, and a route this length gives a reactive dog a lot of opportunities to meet something.

If your dog is early in their work, this is not the one to start with.

Why go anyway

Because it is genuinely beautiful, and because a dog who has done the work deserves somewhere worth walking. The ancient oaks are extraordinary, the woodland smells are on a different scale to a field, and ten miles of varied ground is the kind of day a confident dog will sleep off happily.

Treat it as a goal rather than a starting point. It is the sort of walk that belongs in stage four, when the things you actually wanted become possible again.
