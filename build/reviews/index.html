Helping our anxious border collie

Flo raised concerns about their rescue collie’s running gait, which led the owners to investigate with their vet. The dog turned out to have hip dysplasia and a fractured tooth. With the tooth removed she is, in her owner’s words, a different dog and already much happier.

Lyn Kent — Trustpilot

I wish there were more stars for this review

Two dogs who barked at every visitor and settled only reluctantly. Flo worked with the dogs and the owner together, sent a written summary of each session with things to try, and stayed reachable by text between visits. The owner describes the difference as astonishing.
