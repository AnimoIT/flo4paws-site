Dog Walker Guide

1. Getting started

Flo adds you to the app as a dog walker and links you to each dog you walk. A dog is linked only once its owner has agreed that you can see its walk notes and contact details, and that your walk reports go to Flo and to them.

You then get a Welcome email with a link to set your password. The link works for 7 days; if it runs out, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen, or ask Flo to send the email again. Sign in at app.flo4paws.co.uk in your phone's browser with your email address and password, and you go straight to your walks. To keep it on your phone like an app, use your browser's Add to Home Screen.

Sign out is at the top right.

2. Your dogs

Home lists the dogs you are linked to, with each dog's breed and your last walk with it. A dog with a walk you have started but not sent shows Walk open. Tap a dog to open its card.

Below your dogs, Appointments you are on lists the coming appointments Flo has added you to (for example a session where Flo wants to see a walk): the date and time, the dog, who it is with, and where it is or a Join on Zoom link. A cancelled one is greyed out and marked Cancelled. You can't change or cancel an appointment yourself; message Flo.

3. A dog's card

Everything you need for the walk, in this order:

  • Start a walk (or Continue the open walk).
  • Walk notes from the practice: how to walk this dog. Lead or harness, muzzle, recall, whether it may go off lead, triggers to avoid, what to do if it is scared, and approved routes and times. Read these every time; Flo may change them.
  • Practise on walks: any homework Flo wants practised on walks.
  • Contacts and access: the owner's name and phone number (tap to call), the address, access notes, parking, the vet (tap to call) and the dog's breed, sex, age and colour, as far as Flo has them.
  • Shared thread about <dog>, if Flo has added you to it (see Messages).
  • Notes from the practice: the notes from the dog's appointments, with any document, photo or video on them. Flo can keep a note from you.
  • Your recent walks: the walk reports you have sent. Under each one, Replies opens the conversation about that walk with the owner and Flo: read what they asked, and reply. You get a notification and an email when someone replies, and they are told when you do. You can edit or delete your own replies.

4. On a walk

Select Start a walk as you set off. A timer runs, and the walk notes stay to hand under Walk notes. Select End walk when you are back; you can fill in the report before or after.

If you started a walk by mistake, Remove this walk (started by mistake) at the bottom removes it, with any photos on it. Only a walk you have not sent can be removed.

5. The walk report

Fill in what you can; every part is optional:

  • Walk: Solo or Group.
  • Toilet and water: Wee, Poo and Water, Yes or No (tap again to clear).
  • Where you went.
  • Anything that worried <dog>?, How <dog> reacted and What you did.
  • The dog's own rating scales, if Flo uses them for this dog (1 to 5; tap again to clear).
  • Notes for the practice and owner.
  • Photos and videos: Add photos or videos, up to 10 on a walk; ✕ removes one.

Save for now keeps what you have written. Send walk report ends the walk if it is still running and sends the report: Flo and the owner (and anyone sharing the dog with them) see it in the app straight away, and get an email. Once sent, a report can't be changed or removed in the app: if something is wrong, message Flo.

A walk you leave unsent can be changed for 7 days after you started it.

Don't put key safe numbers, alarm codes or door codes in a report, a note or a message.

6. Messages

If Flo has added you to a dog's shared thread, its card has a Shared thread about <dog> button. Everyone caring for the dog can read it: the owner, anyone sharing the dog with them, Flo, and anyone else Flo has added. Use it for things everyone should know; type in Message and select Send. Messages from Flo are marked (practice).

There is no private message line to Flo in the walks app. For anything else, contact Flo as you usually do.

7. What you can and can't see

You see only the dogs Flo has linked to you, and only what is on their cards. You don't see prices or payments, the owner's forms or consents, their diary, their photos and videos, other dogs, or any other client.

A dog leaves your list when Flo unlinks you or the owner leaves the practice. Walk reports you have already sent stay with Flo and the owner.

8. Emails you'll receive

  • Welcome, with the link to set your password.
  • For an appointment Flo adds you to: the booking email with a calendar file, the reminder before it, any Zoom link, and an email if it moves or is cancelled.
  • New messages in a dog's shared thread you are in.

You don't get emails about the owner's diary.

Back to top

9. Need help?

For anything about a dog or a walk, message Flo or contact them as you usually do. For a problem signing in, use Forgot password, or ask Flo to send the Welcome email again.
