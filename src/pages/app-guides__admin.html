1. Signing in

Go to app.flo4paws.co.uk/admin.html (or sign in at the main address: staff and admin accounts are taken to the portal, clients to their app). Enter your email and password and select Sign In.

Forgotten your password? Select Forgot your password? and follow the link in the email (it works for 7 days). There is no change-password screen in the portal: use the same link to set a new one.

To sign out, select your name at the bottom of the menu, then Sign out. This signs you out of every tab in that browser.

2. Finding your way around

The menu on the left takes you round the portal. An orange number beside an item counts things waiting for you. Consultations can show two: appointments in the next 7 days, and a darker one for booking requests (you have online requests switched off, so this one stays empty).

The 🔔 bell at the top right shows your notifications: new diary entries, messages, forms and so on. A red number counts the unread ones and updates every couple of minutes. Opening the bell shows your latest 30 and marks them read.

If you click outside a pop-up after typing in it, the portal asks Discard your changes? so you don't lose your work by accident.

Admins and staff. Staff can do the day-to-day work. Only admins can: add or change services, booking settings, travel and packages; set other people's availability or time blocks; manage the Team; see Payments and mark a payment unpaid; change the email switches; correct a client's email on an intake submission; and see Activity.

3. The dashboard

The tiles at the top count entries Pending Review, Assessments waiting, Total Entries, Health Forms (all of them) and your Dogs. Select a tile to go to that page. (Pending and Total count the latest 100 diary entries.)

Below are Consultations — Next 7 Days (the whole practice's, not just yours), Needs Review (up to five entries waiting), Recent Health Forms and Pending Assessments. Each has View all.

⏰ Overdue Diaries lists clients whose dog has rating scales but no diary entry for 48 hours. Select Send reminder to email them "A gentle reminder about <dog>'s diary". After a reminder the row says how long ago it went; you can send another after 24 hours. A client who has opted out shows opted out, and one with no email shows no email.

4. Clients

Clients lists everyone with an account. Use Search people or dogs… to find someone by name, email or dog name, and the All / Clients / Co-owners / Physios filters. Each row shows the person's contact details and dogs; a dashed dog chip marks one they co-own or treat as a physio.

The buttons on each row: ✏️ Edit, Add Dog, 📸 Media (with a number when there is media to review), 📧 Welcome (emails a set-your-password link) and 🗑 Delete.

Add a client

Most clients arrive through your intake form (see Intake). To add someone yourself, select + Add Client and choose a role: Client (an owner), Co-owner (someone sharing an existing dog) or Physio (a physiotherapist working with one of your dogs). Give a first and last name and an email or mobile number (one is enough). For a client, you can name their first dog; for a co-owner or physio, choose which dog to link them to. Then select Create Account and use 📧 Welcome on their row to send them a link to set their password.

Edit a client

Select ✏️ Edit to change a client's name, email, mobile, address and postcode, and Availability for first appointment (from their intake form; the client can see it). Select Save Changes.

Add a dog for a client

Select Add Dog on the client's row, give its name, species (Dog, Horse, Cat or Other), breed, gender and date of birth, and select Add Dog.

Client media and share links

Select 📸 Media to see everything shared for that client: photos and videos they upload, and those attached to diary entries. Filter by All, Photos, Videos, Pending or Diary. What the client uploads arrives as pending; select ✓ Review to mark it seen. Use ✎ to give an item a title and a description the client will see.

+ Upload for Client adds photos or videos yourself (JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 10 files of 100 MB each); you choose the dog and the client gets an email.

To send some of it to someone without an account, such as a vet or farrier, select 🔗 Select to share, tick the items and select Create share link, then Copy the link. The person sees just those photos and videos, grouped by dog. A link can't be added to later, and it works until you Revoke it. Every link is listed on the Share Links page.

5. Dogs

Dogs lists every dog with its owner, species, breed, gender and date of birth. Filter by Horse, Dog, Cat or Other, or search by name. + Add Dog adds one for any client. Each row has ✏️ Edit, 🗑 Delete, 📄 Vet & physio notes, 📊 Table view and 🕒 Timeline, and 💬 Thread when the dog has a shared thread (see Messages).

Edit a dog

Select ✏️ Edit. The form holds the dog's details and the working parts of its plan:

  • Details: name, species, breed, gender, date of birth, age and weight.
  • Visit details: a visit address and postcode if the dog is kept somewhere other than the client's address, access notes and parking.
  • Vet: vet name, phone, email and address.
  • Homework: things for the client to work on. It appears at the top of the client's plan on their Home screen, and they get an email when it changes (see the note below).
  • Co-owners: link other people to this dog. They see it in their app and can log diary entries for it. A physio added here gets it on their caseload; tick In the shared thread to let them see and post in the dog's shared thread.
  • Package: start one of your packages (Core support, Ongoing support) for this dog, add its charge, mark it paid, extend it or end it. Sessions under a package are not counted or limited. The client's Home shows "Part of your package" on an appointment under it; any travel or extra price you set on that appointment still shows.
  • Exercises: prescribe exercises from your Library, with reps or time, how often and notes. Each shows how many sessions the client has logged and how many are waiting for review; select that line to open them.
  • Diary Rating Scales: choose which 1–5 scales the client rates in each diary entry. Switch each On or Off, use Labels to describe each level, and + Add custom scale to make your own.

Save Changes saves the details and homework. Co-owners, exercises, the package and rating scales save as soon as you change them.

The client is told when the plan changes. When you change the homework (to new text, not clearing it) or add an exercise, the owner and any co-owners get one email and one notification, "<your first name> at Flo4Paws has updated <dog>'s plan", about 10 minutes after the last change (never more than 30 minutes after the first). A physio adding an exercise sends it too, in their name. Editing an exercise already on the plan sends nothing, and you are never sent it yourself. If a deploy happens during those 10 minutes that one message is lost. To stop these emails, switch off Homework or exercises updated in Settings › Emails.

The scale labels window lets you describe each level so the client knows what to pick, for example "Alert for a minute" for a 3.

Vet & physio notes

Select 📄 Vet & physio notes on a row. Notes are kept on two tabs, Vet notes and Physio notes, each with a count. To add one, open the right tab, choose a PDF or photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB) and select Upload. Notes are visible to the client and co-owners (and a linked physio, who can read them but not add them), and the client can add their own from the app. Adding a note sends no email.

Table view

📊 Table view shows the dog's diary as a table: date, time, event, trigger and reaction, one column per rating scale, details, status and replies, with an average for each scale. Choose a date range, and (once there are exercise sessions) show diary entries, sessions or both. ⬇ Download CSV saves it for a spreadsheet. Click a row to open the entry.

Timeline

🕒 Timeline lists everything that has happened for the dog, newest first and grouped by month: appointments, payments, packages, homework changes, diary entries, forms, media and messages, with who did it. Filter by type and dates, select a line to open it, and use 🖨 Print / PDF or ⬇ Download CSV to keep a copy.

6. Homework & exercises

This page lists every dog with its owner, homework (the first line), number of exercises, when a session was last logged and its package, with days left. Search by name or owner, or tick No homework yet to find dogs still waiting for theirs.

Select ✏️ Set to write or change the homework (Save homework) and add, edit or remove exercises in one place. The client gets the "plan updated" email described above.

7. Diary entries

Diary Entries groups entries by dog, with the number of entries, how many are exercise sessions, and how many are pending. Use the All / Pending / Reviewed / Flagged tabs, the type list (diary entries or exercise sessions), and search.

Select a dog to see its entries, with 📊 Table view and Review or Delete on each.

Review an entry

Select Review. You see the event, trigger and reaction, details, any photos or videos, the ratings, and the Discussion. Type in Write a reply… and select Send to reply; the client gets an email. You can edit (✎) or delete (🗑) any reply. Add an optional Your Note for the client, then ✓ Mark Reviewed, or ⚠️ Flag if it needs following up.

The rating scales for the dog can be changed here too, the same as in Edit Dog.

Review an exercise session

When a client logs an exercise from their plan it arrives as an exercise session, marked EXERCISE. It shows how the session went and any photos. There is no note box: your reply is the review. Sending a reply marks the session reviewed and the client gets one email.

Whether the client is emailed when you mark an entry reviewed or flagged is set in Settings › Emails. You have Diary entry reviewed or flagged switched off for clients, so they see the change in the app only.

8. Health forms and assessments

Health Forms lists the forms clients have sent (source Client) and PDFs you have uploaded (Admin upload). Clients send one from their Profile. Select View to read one, then ✓ Mark Reviewed (the client gets an email) or 🖨️ Print / Save PDF. + Upload Form adds a PDF for a client and dog.

Assessments lists Comfort & Movement Assessments: 20 questions about comfort and movement, scored out of 60 (higher means fewer concerns), with photos from eight angles and three short videos. Select View, then ✓ Mark Reviewed or ⚑ Flag for Follow-up (either one sends the client "Your assessment has been reviewed"). 🔗 Share with vet makes a link to the photos and videos.

9. Consultations and the calendar

Consultations is your booking calendar. Switch between List, Day and Week, move with ‹ Today › or the date box, and choose All staff or one person. The legend shows each person's colour, the time kept clear after an appointment, time blocks, time not working and cancelled appointments.

On a phone, Week becomes a day-by-day list and Day shows one person at a time.

Marks on an appointment: £ means unpaid, ✓ completed, and a striped block after it is the time kept clear.

Book an appointment

Select + Appointment (top right, in every view). Choose the client, the dog (optional), the service and who it is with (only people who offer that service are listed), then the date and start time. The form checks the time against working hours, other appointments and time blocks: ✓ No clashes, or a warning with Add anyway.

If the dog has a package running on that date, Under <package> is already ticked, so the appointment is part of the package and has no separate price. Untick it for an extra you will charge for.

Add a location and notes for the client if you like, and leave Notify the client ticked to send them an email with a calendar file (co-owners get it too) and, later, the 24-hour reminder. If you untick it, neither is sent. Select Add appointment.

Appointment details and payment

Select an appointment to open it: client and dog, who with, when, where and the status (Scheduled, Completed or No-show; set Completed after the session). The Payment box shows the price and whether it is paid: Set price, Mark paid… (card, bank transfer, cash or other, and the date), Change price or Remove price. Card payments through the app are not switched on yet, so record payments here as they come in.

Move, cancel or delete

At the bottom of an appointment, Client messages lists the emails for it (sent, due, skipped or failed).

Move / edit changes the time, person or details; tick Tell the client about the new time to email them. Cancel appointment keeps the record and frees the time: choose a reason and whether to tell the client. Delete is only for mistakes. Clients can also cancel appointments booked in the calendar themselves, in the app, until they start; you get "Client cancelled" (it says if it was inside your notice period).

Time blocks

+ Time block keeps time clear: choose who (admins can block time for anyone), a reason (Time off, Travelling, Meeting, CPD / training, Other), the dates, all day or a start and end time, and a staff-only note. Repeat weekly until repeats it every week for up to a year. If the block covers an appointment you are warned before it is added.

After the session

Select Notes on the appointment to write up the session. Add photos or videos and a document if you like, then Save Notes & Notify Client: the appointment is marked Completed, and the client gets "Consultation notes available: <title>" and can read them in the app.

The list view

List shows Upcoming, and Past appointments, with filters for one client and for upcoming, all or past.

10. Booking set-up

Booking set-up (in Settings) has four tabs: Services, Team availability, Packages and Settings.

Services

Each service has a length, a buffer kept clear after it, the price as clients see it, and who offers it. Select ✏️ Edit (or + Add service; admins only) to set the name, a description clients see, length, buffer, price text, where it happens (home or yard visit, online, clinic or phone), a colour, which animals it is for, who offers it, whether it is active and whether clients can request it online.

Team availability

Choose a person to set their job title, calendar colour, whether they can be booked, their working hours for each day (up to six time ranges a day) and the services they offer. Staff can change their own hours; admins can change anyone's.

Packages

Your packages are listed with their length, price, what is included and whether they are on sale. + Add package or Edit (admins) sets the name, price, length (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 or 12 months) and what's included, one item per line. Switch a package off to stop selling it; packages already running carry on. Start one for a dog from Edit Dog. Two weeks before a package ends, staff and admins (not physios) get "<dog>'s <package> ends in N days".

Settings

Cancellation notice (how long before an appointment a client can cancel without the late warning; yours is 48 hours) and the cancellation policy clients see. You have no policy text yet, so clients see only the notice period.

Online booking requests are off, so clients ask to change appointments by message. Travel charges are not set up. Both can be switched on here if you want them.

11. Messages

Messages lists conversations with clients, newest first; a dot marks unread. Select one to open it. Type a message and press Enter to send (Shift+Enter for a new line), or attach a file with 📎 (up to 25 MB). ✓✓ means the client has read it. The list refreshes every 30 seconds.

Shared dog threads

A dog with co-owners or a physio has its own shared thread, listed under Dog threads. Everyone caring for that dog sees it: the owner, co-owners, physios you have ticked In the shared thread in Edit Dog, and the practice. Use it for things everyone should know; keep private matters in the client's own conversation. 💬 Thread on the Dogs list and on a dog's entries opens it too.

12. Intake

When someone fills in your intake form, they are emailed a link to set up their account (it works for 7 days) and you are told. Until they use it, the submission waits here, showing everything they sent: their details, each dog, and the consents they agreed to.

  • Print prints that submission, or saves it as a PDF.
  • Resend link (called Send a new link once the old one has run out) emails a fresh link that works for 7 days. Any earlier link stops working.
  • Change email (admins) corrects a mistyped address and sends a new link to it. It can be changed up to five times, and not to an address that already has an account.
  • Delete removes a submission you don't want.

Once the client sets their password, the submission becomes their account, with their dogs, answers and consents, and leaves this list.

13. Share links

Share Links lists every link made from a client's media or with Share with vet on an assessment: its label, how many items, when it was made, whether it is active, and the link. Select Revoke to turn one off; the page then tells anyone who opens it that the link has been turned off.

14. Settings

Select your name at the bottom of the menu to open Settings.

Intake form

Choose the questions on your intake form: tick Show, change the wording, add an example answer or a default answer, and choose which species a question is for (none ticked means every species). Some questions are required on every form, or other questions depend on them, and can't be hidden. Add a question adds your own. Changes are saved as you go but reach clients only when you select Publish to my live form.

Consents

The consents clients agree to on the intake form, each per client or per dog. Some are always asked; others you can choose to ask. Edit wording, then Publish version N. A published version can't be changed or unpublished: each change is a new version, and clients' answers keep the version they agreed to. New wording, and ticking Ask this on the form, reach clients only after you select Publish to my live form on the Intake form page.

Library

Your exercise library: name, description and an optional video link. + Add Resource adds one; Edit or Remove (prescriptions already given keep working, and Show removed resources lets you restore it).

Team

Admins see the team: name, email, role and whether each person has a calendar link. + Add Team Member creates a staff or admin account and shows a temporary password once, to pass on. Deactivate stops someone signing in; Reactivate brings them back. Physios are listed here too.

Payments

Admins see what has been received in a period and what is unpaid now (appointments and packages). Choose this month, last month, this tax year or your own dates, and Download for my books (CSV). Card payments through the app (Stripe) are not switched on yet.

Emails

Choose which emails go out, for each type and each group: Client, Physio and Practice. A dash means that group never gets that email. Switching one off stops the email only and the notification in the app still appears, except for the three marked as a whole feature (the 24-hour reminder, the rebook email and the diary reminder), which stop altogether. Account emails (welcome, password reset, registration links) are always sent. Under Your own emails you can have practice emails as they happen, or one summary a day at about 6pm. Only admins can change the switches; they select Save changes.

Activity

Admins can see sign-ins (successful and failed) for today, the last 7 days or the last 30 days.

My calendar link

Select Create my calendar link to add your bookings to your own calendar app (Google, Apple or Outlook): copy the link and follow the instructions shown. Reset link makes a new one and stops the old; Remove link turns it off.
