How to use the Flo4Paws app: setting up your account, keeping your dog’s diary, sharing photos and vet notes, and keeping in touch with Flo between sessions.

The app and app.flo4paws.co.uk are the same thing, so everything here works on your phone or in a browser. Screenshots show an example dog, Bramble. To keep a copy of this guide, use your browser’s Print and choose Save as PDF.

1. Getting started

Setting up takes three short steps: fill in Flo’s health and history form, set a password from the email that follows, then sign in.

Fill in the form

The form has five steps: About you, Your animals, About each dog, Consent and Review. Select Continue to move on, or Back to change an earlier step. The panel on the right shows who and what the form covers so far.

  • About you. Your first name, last name and email address are required. Flo uses your email to set up your account.
  • Your animals. Give each dog’s name and what kind of animal they are, and their breed if you know it. Select Add another dog if you have more than one.
  • About each dog. Their background, the behaviour you’d like help with, their health and vet, and their daily life. Answer what you can; anything you leave blank shows as “Not answered”.
  • Consent. Read each one and answer Yes or No. A few are required: if you answer No to those, the form can’t be sent and Flo will talk to you directly instead.
  • Review. Check your answers, then select Send my details.

Once it’s sent you’ll see a thank-you page, and Flo will be in touch to arrange your free discovery call.

Set your password

Straight after you send the form, you get an email called Complete your Flo4Paws registration. Open the link in it; it works for 7 days. If it isn’t in your inbox, check your spam folder.

Choose a password of at least 8 characters, including a letter and a number, type it twice and select Create my account. Your account is then ready: select Sign in to use the app in your browser, or install it with Get the iPhone app or Get the Android app.

If the link has run out, ask Flo to send you a new one.

Signing in

Open the app, or go to app.flo4paws.co.uk, and sign in with your email address and password. If you forget your password, select Forgot your password? and follow the link in the email.

2. Home

Home is the first screen after you sign in. From here you can:

  • start a New Diary Entry or Add Media with the two tiles at the top;
  • see your Recent Entries, and how many are still waiting for Flo to review;
  • see My Consultations, your upcoming sessions. Tap one to see the date, time, location and which dog it’s for;
  • work through Your Exercises, if Flo has set you any (see section 4).

The bar at the bottom takes you to Home, Diary, Media, Messages and Profile.

The bell at the top shows your notifications, for example when Flo has replied to a diary entry or booked a session. A red number means you have new ones.

3. Your dog’s diary

The diary is where you tell Flo how things are going between sessions. A few lines most days is plenty. Open the Diary tab and fill in New Entry:

  • Choose the dog and the date, and pick the time of day.
  • Describe the event (what you were doing), the trigger (what set it off, if anything) and the reaction (what your dog did).
  • Answer the rating scales. Flo sets these up for your dog, such as Barking or Noise sensitivity, so that you can both see the pattern over time. Pick the level that fits, from Very Low to Very High. Some scales carry Flo’s own description under each level. Scales marked with a red star need an answer.
  • Add any details, and attach photos or videos if they help (up to 10 files, 100 MB each).
  • Select Submit Entry.

Your entries appear under Past Entries, with your ratings as coloured numbers from 1 (green) to 5 (red), and on Home under Recent Entries. Once Flo has read an entry it shows Reviewed. Tap an entry to open it: Flo’s replies appear under Discussion, and you can answer in Write a reply… and select Send.

4. Exercises

If Flo, or a physiotherapist working with her, sets your dog an exercise, it appears on Home under Your Exercises. Each one shows how many repetitions (or how long) and how often, with Watch video where there is one.

After you’ve done an exercise, record it:

  • Select + Log session on the exercise.
  • Choose the time of day: Morning, Afternoon or Evening.
  • Optionally, say how it went, and add up to 10 photos or videos.
  • Select Save session.

The exercise then shows how many sessions you’ve logged and when the latest was.

Back to top

5. Photos, videos and vet notes

The Media tab holds the photos and videos you’ve shared. Filter them by Photos, Videos, Pending or Reviewed, and select + Add Media to upload more (up to 10 files, 100 MB each). Body language on a walk or a moment at the window often tells Flo more than a description.

Further down, Vet notes is where letters and reports from your vet go. Choose the dog, then + Add vet note to upload a PDF or a photo (up to 20 MB). Flo can see it straight away.

Back to top

6. Messages

Messages is a direct line to Flo. Type in Message your practitioner… and select Send. Use the paperclip button to attach a file. Flo’s replies appear in the same conversation, and two ticks under your message mean it has been delivered.

Back to top

7. Your profile

Profile shows your name, how many diary entries and dogs you have, and how many entries have been reviewed. From here you can open:

  • My Diary and My Media;
  • My Health Forms: the forms you’ve sent and their status. Flo can see all of these;
  • Submit New Health Form: to update Flo with new information about your dog;
  • Comfort & Movement Assessment: a short posture assessment;
  • Notifications.
