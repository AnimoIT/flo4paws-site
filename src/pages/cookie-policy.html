Cookie policyLast updated: September 2026

This policy explains what this website and the Flo4Paws platform store in your browser, and how to change your choice.

1. This website (www.flo4paws.co.uk)

When you first visit, a bar at the foot of the page asks whether you accept cookies. Until you choose, and if you decline, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. Google may still receive anonymous, cookie-free signals that a page was viewed (Google Consent Mode), which cannot identify you.

If you accept, the site sets Google Analytics cookies (names beginning _ga, kept for up to two years) so Flo can see which pages are read and how people arrive, and Google Ads can measure whether an advert led to an enquiry. Your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage under the key f4p-consent so you are not asked again on every page.

2. The Flo4Paws platform (app.flo4paws.co.uk)

The platform does not use traditional cookies. It stores a secure refresh token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between sessions. The platform does not use advertising, third-party tracking, analytics or social media cookies.

3. How to clear stored data

Clear your browser's site data or cache. On a mobile device, clear app data in your device settings. Doing so will sign you out of Flo4Paws and will ask for your cookie choice again on this website.
