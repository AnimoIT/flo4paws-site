This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage are used.

1. What we use

The Flo4Paws platform does not use traditional cookies. It stores a secure refresh token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between sessions.

2. What the platform does not use

  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Analytics cookies
  • Social media cookies

This section describes the platform at app.flo4paws.co.uk. It does not yet describe this website. When analytics or advertising tags are added here, this policy must be updated and a consent banner added before those tags may load.

3. How to clear stored data

Clear your browser's site data or cache. On a mobile device, clear app data in your device settings. Doing so will sign you out of Flo4Paws.
